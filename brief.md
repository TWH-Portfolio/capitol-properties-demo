## References used

### 1. RealManage (realmanage.com)
- Hero composition: Bold centered value proposition with quantified proof ("22+ years", "3,500+ communities")
- Section order: Problem-solution, service breadth, differentiators, social proof
- Copy voice: Conversational yet authoritative, short punchy sentences, directly addresses pain
- Photo treatment: Minimal, reserving photography for high-impact moments
- Trust signals: Specific numbers (22+ years, 50+ offices, 3,500+ communities, 2.3M residents)
- Conversion path: "Get started" CTA, demo request forms, phone
- **Apply to this build:** Stats strip with specific management numbers, problem-first hero that names the owner's pain, service cards organized by audience (owners vs tenants)

### 2. Buildium (buildium.com)
- Hero composition: Direct value prop with immediate action path
- Section order: Benefits to concrete features to social proof
- Copy voice: Action-oriented, no jargon, accessible
- Photo treatment: Clean UI mockups, focused compositions
- Trust signals: "#1 most recommended", customer testimonials with unit counts, 95% satisfaction, founded 2004
- Conversion path: Free trial CTA, specific outcome promises
- **Apply to this build:** Testimonials with property-count attribution, clean section transitions, outcome-focused service descriptions rather than feature lists

### Cross-reference design thesis
Lead with the owner's pain point (vacancy, maintenance headaches, late rent) in the hero, then resolve with specific services. Use a warm professional tone that feels like talking to a competent property manager, not reading a brochure. Stats strip with oddly specific numbers (not round). Testimonials attributed to real owner types (condo owner, single-family investor, etc.).
